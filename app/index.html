    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Seven stages, in the order to play them. Story first, the one missable trophy during Chapter 1, then strangers, collectibles, activities and the money cleanup — 100% completion last. Guides report no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission (Output Lag). Cheats switch trophies and saving off, so keep them off for the whole run (GTABase). There are no online trophies and no difficulty trophies — the list is long, not hard.

      About the list & the sources

      Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list as published 18 November 2026 — 55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze; 28 of them hidden on PlayStation until earned. The list was reported by Push Square from Exophase, and GTABase, PowerPyx, GTA Zone, Output Lag and EGamers all report the same 55 names. Every guide on this page is written in our own words for this guide and names its sources beside it — a source is cited, never copied. Five trophies had no confirmed unlock method at launch (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) and their guides say so plainly instead of inventing a method. One trophy is flagged possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude (GTABase / PowerPyx).
